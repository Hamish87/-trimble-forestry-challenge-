# Holztransport · Carrier-first — research notes

Condensed from three research reports compiled on 16 October 2026 for the demo in `transport/` (see `SPEC.md` for how
they are cited as **[ELDAT]**, **[RES]** and `RESEARCH §1–§3`, and `PLAN.md` Phase 0). Markers are kept from the
reports: **[primary]** = read in the standard's own schema or spreadsheet, a law text, a framework agreement, a state-forest
contract document, an official press release, or a live `curl`/API check on 16 Oct 2026; **[secondary]** = trade press,
vendor listing, glossary, search-engine excerpt or inference; **[unverified]** = could not be confirmed on a primary page;
**[old]** = source older than about six years. German terms are glossed on first use. Where the reports disagree, both
values are given with their sources. Nothing here is a statement of LOGIBALL or Trimble capabilities.

## Executive summary

1. ELDAT (ELektronischer DATenstandard für Holzdaten) is the German forest-to-mill data standard in two live branches: ELDATclassic (CSV/XML record types, V2.1.1 of 26.09.2024) and ELDATsmart (JSON Schema draft-07, v1.0.4 of 24.02.2025), governed by a parity ELDAT-Beirat and administered by KWF and AGR; the schema is public on GitHub, no XSD exists [primary].
2. ELDATsmart has five modules (Holzbereitstellung, Transportauftrag, Lieferschein, Messprotokoll, Abrechnung), each ending in a status container whose codes 10–150 include 50 Angenommen, 60 Abgelehnt, 110 Fahre ins Revier, 120 Lieferschein erstellt, 140 Am Lieferort angekommen — the vocabulary the demo shows beside its load states [primary].
3. The Polter (roadside log pile) is the core unit: one coordinate pair in decimal degrees plus Polternummer, identifiers travelling as typed `wood_id` (HAB, Los, Verkaufslos, Polter, Polter-GUID) and `ref_type` keys per party; the transport order (TA/PTR/AFK in classic) and delivery note (LSK/LSP/LSN) reference them [primary].
4. No statute prescribes a Lieferschein (delivery note); § 7 GüKG requires an on-board document naming goods, loading/unloading place and Auftraggeber (electronic allowed); § 408 HGB lists the optional Frachtbrief content; the practical minimum for sawlogs is RVWV Anlage 8.15 (in force 01.10.2025), which the demo's print follows alongside the BaySF form fields [primary].
5. § 34 StVZO caps truck combinations at 40 t; 44 t for timber exists only as time-limited § 70 StVZO exemptions for Schadholz (damaged timber), e.g. Baden-Württemberg to 31.12.2027 per its press release (31.03.2027 per an AGR overview), lapsing when exceeded [primary]/[secondary].
6. Units per RVR 2020: Fm o.R., Rm m.R., SRm, t atro; Rm→Fm 0,70/0,65/0,60 for 1/2/3 m pieces; 1 t atro ≙ 2,2 Fm Fichte/Tanne, 2,1 Kiefer, 1,5 Buche, 1,4 Eiche; conversions are "vorrangig statistisch" and "als Verkaufsmaß nicht anzuwenden", and ELDAT carries factors per Hieb — hence the demo's versioned, labelled factors [primary].
7. Hierarchy differs by state: ForstBW Holzlistennummer + Polter-Nr + Los + Abfuhrfreigabe; Rheinland-Pfalz HAB-Los + Kaufvertrags-/Vorzeigungsnummer; BaySF Partie (buyer-agreed billing unit) + HAB/Los + Kd-Partie-Nr; "Ladenummer" is an unsourced plant-side alias [primary]/[unverified].
8. State GIS back-ends use ETRS89/UTM zone 32 (EPSG:25832) in BW, BY (default) and NRW while ELDAT uses WGS 84; printed lists show "32U 461344 5481745", zone-prefixed 8-digit eastings, Gauß-Krüger 7-digit Rechtswerte and DMS with "O" for east — the demo's own parser covers all of these with a plausibility decision table [primary].
9. EUDR applies to wood from 30.12.2026 (Reg. 2025/2650; small/micro 30.06.2027); forest owners file a one-time simplified declaration and pass reference/verification numbers to the first buyer; ELDAT 1.0.4/2.1.1 carry both numbers per Polter, so a carrier's delivery note is expected to transport them [primary].
10. Adoption caveat: in a 2022 survey of state forest enterprises 87 % used no data standard for the Transportauftrag and 93 % none for the Lieferschein; orders reach hauliers mostly as e-mail PDFs with a map, re-typed by hand — the premise of the demo's PDF-first inbox [primary].
11. Market: Timber Flow (LOGIBALL/Localion, Herne) is the only product starting from the PDF inbox (LITE €0 / ADVANCED €24 / PRO €44 per vehicle per month plus a separately priced Localion Navigator plan); WASP, Latschbacher, Forstware and Trimble Zenith/CFBusiness are mill- or cooperative-centred; Trimble has no European carrier-first product [primary]/[secondary].
12. German hauliers are tiny — Bavaria 278 firms, mean 4,3 trucks, 66 % with ≤3 trucks, ~12 regular customers each, orders 42 % industry / 30 % forest owners / 21 % trade — with documented pain points: mill queues up to an hour (unpaid), wrong Polter coordinates causing unpaid search trips, paper Lieferscheine faxed daily [primary].
13. Technical: Leaflet 1.9.4 is the buildless map library of record and MapLibre 6.14.0 is ESM-only; OSM tiles, basemap.de (CC BY 4.0) and the BW/BY/NRW orthophoto WMS endpoints are key-free with CORS verified; proj4js 2.22.0 needs the EPSG:1777 Helmert (≈3 m) for Gauß-Krüger because it cannot load the BETA2007 grid [primary].
14. pdf.js 6.4.299 is ES-module only and 3.11.174 is the last classic-script build (the one the demo loads); Tesseract.js 7.0.0 would cost ≈5,3 MB on first German OCR, which is why the demo routes scans to manual capture instead [primary].
15. Offline pattern: client UUID event ids used as idempotency keys, separate event/receipt times, append-only outbox, conflicts surfaced for a human instead of last-write-wins; Background Sync is still Chromium-only, and Document-AI vendors all pair each field with page + box + confidence — the shape the demo's review screen mirrors [primary].

## §1 ELDAT and German document practice

### 1.1 ELDAT: what it is, who maintains it, versions
- ELDAT dates from 2002; ELDATsmart became the official industry standard when the Plattform Forst & Holz signed the Rahmenvereinbarung für ELDAT (RVE) in April 2018; the RVE "expressly recommends" .eldat and has no force of law. [primary] https://www.dfwr.de/projekte/eldat-smart/ · https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/eldat/ · https://www.kwh40.de/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Standpunkt_Datenstandards_Wald_Holz40_v1.0.pdf
- Governance: ELDAT-Beirat (8 members, parity forestry/timber industry) configures; KWF and AGR (Arbeitsgemeinschaft Rohholz) administer; KWF has run the secretariat since 2004. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Pressemitteilung-ELDAT-02-2025.pdf · https://digitalisierung.fnr.de/datenstandards/eldatsmart
- Branches: ELDATclassic "Version 2.1.1, Stand 26.09.2024" (record types, CSV/XML) and ELDATsmart 1.0.4 (JSON, 24.02.2025: EUDR reference/verification numbers mandatory, optional geocoordinates, Latin species names, CO2 fields per ISO 13391). There is no "ELDAT 2.x smart"; 2.x is classic. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls · https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/1.0.4/schema_de.json · https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/FTI_02_2025-final.pdf
- Schema: JSON Schema draft-07, 224 definitions, repo GPL-3.0, branches 1.0.1–1.0.4 (no tags); 1.0.3→1.0.4 adds `carbon_footprint_*`, `eudr_dds_json`, `trans_price_text`, removes nothing. No public XSD for either branch [verified absence]. [primary] https://github.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema · https://api.github.com/repos/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/branches
- Transport of files: JSON with extension `.eldat`, e-mail attachment or "andere elektronische Austauschwege"; external files (photos, maps) travel as `other_file{path, file_text}` references, not embedded; envelope `document.meta` carries GUID `transaction_id`/`process_id`/`module_id`. No API spec is published; the protocol page is 404. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/20210119_Nutzerleitfaden_fuer_ELDATsmart.docx
- Reach claims disagree: "Etwa 80 % des Rundholzes" via ELDAT (KWF press release 2025) vs ~60 % national coverage (KWF podcast via FNR). [primary]/[secondary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Pressemitteilung-ELDAT-02-2025.pdf · https://digitalisierung.fnr.de/datenstandards/eldatsmart

### 1.2 ELDATsmart modules and status codes
- Record types were dropped deliberately (users picked wrong ones); five nested modules, order free, modules may be omitted or repeated: `wood_allocation` (Holzbereitstellung, provision), `transfer_order` (Transportauftrag), `delivery_note` (Lieferschein), `measurement_journal` (Messprotokoll), `clearing` (Abrechnung). [primary] https://www.fnr.de/fileadmin/projektdatenbank/22022714.pdf
- The Polter is "Ausgangspunkt und konstante Kerneinheit"; it must physically exist, appears once with X/Y and Polternummer; per list either one aggregated description or a single-log list, never both. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/20210119_Nutzerleitfaden_fuer_ELDATsmart.docx
- `status_arr` on every module: `creation_datetime`, `statusid`, `additionalinformation`, sender `coordinate`. Status ids: 10 Erstellt · 20 Geändert · 30 Storniert · 40 Gesendet · **50 Angenommen** · **60 Abgelehnt** · 70 Disponiert · 80 Auftragsbeginn · 90 Unterbrochen · 100 Abgebrochen · **110 Fahre ins Revier** · **120 Lieferschein erstellt** · 130 Verlasse Revier · **140 Am Lieferort angekommen** · 150 Auftragsende. [primary] table "Status-ID / statusid" in https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/20210119_Nutzerleitfaden_fuer_ELDATsmart.docx
- `transfer_order`: required `transfer_address` (Transportauftraggeber, Ladestelle, Entladestelle, Spediteur), `transfer_inf` (`validity_period`, `trans_period_date`, `distance`, `trans_zone`, `trans_price`, `delivery_no_range[]` = number range the haulier may use for delivery notes, `other_file[]`), `pile_data[]` copied 1:1 from the provision (each with `coordinate`* and `eudr_dds_json[]`*), optional `barcode_inf`; loading/unloading places need "mindestens … Koordinaten", no postal address. Intra-company Fuhraufträge (trip orders) are out of scope of the standard. [primary] https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/1.0.4/schema_de.json · https://www.fnr.de/fileadmin/projektdatenbank/22022714.pdf (p.23)
- `delivery_note`: `delivery.delivery_no`*, optional `signature_data` (base64 image), `haulier` (driver surname*, phone, e-mail), `haul_means[]` per vehicle (`haul_means_no`* = Kennzeichen or wagon id, `amount[]`*, `haul_measured`, `eudr_dds_json[]`*). The "Frachtursprung" must state loading time, loaded quantity and the **Restmenge** left at the Polter so the mill can order further loads. The mill confirms by returning the document "Angenommen" (50) or "Abgelehnt" (60); no source requires confirmation before unloading. [primary] https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/1.0.4/schema_de.json · https://www.fnr.de/fileadmin/projektdatenbank/22022714.pdf (pp.24–25) · https://digitalisierung.fnr.de/datenstandards/eldatsmart · https://www.bfh.ch/dam/jcr:9cd4dc0f-96c3-4b33-b40e-ced79a170624/4-3-referat-a-kaulen.pdf
- `measurement_journal`: `industrie_measured`* (Werkmaß: Einzelstamm-Werk, Foto-Optisch, Gravimetrisch, Harvester) each with `measurement_id` (Messprotokollnummer), `calibration{calibration_id, calibrated_until, calibrated_values[]}`; gravimetric and crane records carry a mandatory **hiebsbezogener** `conversion_factor_qm_t` (Fm o.R. per t lutro). [primary] https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/1.0.4/schema_de.json
- Reference tables that matter for a carrier: `role_business` (`spe` Spediteur, `tra` Transportauftraggeber, `lad`/`ent` loading/unloading place, `abn` Abnehmer, `lie` Lieferant), `location_type` (`pol` Polter, `wal` Waldlager, `wer` Werk, `wzw` Werk Zwischenlager, `wva` Vermessungsanlage), `amount_unit` (`fmo`, `rmm`, `srm`, `atr`, `lut`, `cbm`, `lfm`, `stk`, `cft`), `delivery_term` (forestry `uwe` Unfrei Werk … plus Incoterms `fca` Frei Waldstraße, `ddp` Frei Werk), `crs` free string "Gemäß http://www.epsg.org", coordinates in decimal degrees. `business_transaction` `VWa`/`VWe` read as Verkauf nach Waldmaß/Werksmaß [unverified]. [primary] https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/1.0.4/schema_de.json · https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/20210119_Nutzerleitfaden_fuer_ELDATsmart.docx

### 1.3 ELDATclassic record types (ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls, V2.1.1)
- 28 sheets; CSV with `;`, 6 technical key fields per record (Satzart, Staaten-/Landesschlüssel, USt-ID of sender, Schnittstellenkennung, Laufende_Nr); `ADA` is the shipment header. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- **TA Transportauftrag** "an den Holztransporteur": `Transportauftrag_Nr`* "Nr des Abfuhrauftrags", `Holzabnehmer_Adressdaten_ID`* (commercial Auftraggeber), `Transporteur_Kontakt_ID`*, `Abrechnungs_Nr_Abnehmer` "Kundenpartie/Kaufnummer des Käufers", `Vertrag_Nr`, `Lieferbedingungen`, `GUID`*, `Storno`. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- **PTR Polter**: `Polter_Bezeichnung`, `Lagerort`, `Geometrieschluessel_X/Y` ("E010.56.37,4" / "N56.38.24,8"), `Koordinatensystem` "EPSG_4326", `Zahl_Staemme`, `Holzart`, `Guete`, `Sorte`, `Laenge_von/bis`, `Menge_Wert/_Einheit`, `Abfuhrfreigabe_Nr` ("PartieNr.Verkaufsnummer"), `Abfuhrzeitraum_von/bis`, `Ladezone`, `Frachtpreis_pro_Einheit`, `Absprungkoordinate_*` (suggested exit point from the public road). [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- **AFK Abfuhrkarte**: map extent X1/Y1/X2/Y2, scale, dpi, `Karte_Verweis` (file/URL), `GPX_Datei_Verweis` (route recommendations). [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- **LSK Lieferschein Kopfdaten** "(Rückmeldung einer erfolgten Fuhre)": three note numbers `Lieferschein_Nr_TA` (issued by the Transportauftraggeber from LSN ranges), `Lieferschein_Nr_TR` (haulier), `Lieferschein_Nr_EMPF` (receiver's Annahmenummer); `Messprotokoll_Nr` "oder Wiegescheins"; `Fahrer`*, `Fahrzeug_Nr`, `Transportmittel_Nr_1`* (tractor plate), `_Nr_2` (trailer), `Ankunftszeitpunkt`, `Uebergabezeitpunkt`, `Abladezeitpunkt`. **LSP** = positions per Polter, **LSN** = allowed note-number ranges. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- **WDK Werksdaten Kopfdaten**: `Abfuhrfreigabe_Nr`, `Los_Nr`, `Eich_ID` (PTB approval), `Eichung_gueltig_bis`, sorting limits, `RI_AZ_INFFO` bark-deduction info, `FUHR_INFO`. **WDG Gewichtsholz**: `Wiegeschein_Nr`, `_Datum`, `Eichdatum_Fahrzeugwaage_bis`, `Masse` (Abrechnungsmasse), `Masse_1/_2` alternatives (Raummaß, t lutro, t atro). **WDE Einzelstammprotokoll**: `Kauf_Nr` "Interne Nummer des Kunden", `Messprotokoll_Nr`*, `Fahrer`, `LKW_Nr`, `Pol_Kennzeichen_*`, `Fuhrzettel_Nr`, per-log length/diameters/volume and deductions. WDA = aggregated. [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- Coordinates in classic are text in EPSG_4326 with a DMS-like format, uniform since change log 2.1 (19.08.2010). [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls (Änderungshistorie)

### 1.4 Identifiers and typed references
- ELDATsmart `wood_id_type`: `hab` HAB-Nummer, `hal` HAB+Los, `los`, `vlo` Verkaufslos, `pol` Polternummer, `poa` Polter-Auftragsnummer, `pog` Polter-GUID, `sta` Stammnummer, `arb`, `art`. `ref_type`: `afn` Abfuhrfreigabenummer, `hin` Hiebsnummer, `vzn` Vorzeigungsnummer, `kas` Kassenzeichen, contract/reference numbers per party (`vha`/`rha` Holzabnehmer, `vli`/`rli` Lieferant, `vsp`/`rsp` Spediteur, `vta`/`rta` Transportauftraggeber, `vka`/`rka` Holzkäufer), `aec`/`aew`/`ael`/`aes` Anliefernummer per container/wagon/Lagerbox/other. [primary] https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/1.0.4/schema_de.json · https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/20210119_Nutzerleitfaden_fuer_ELDATsmart.docx
- Reading: Kauf-Nr/Partie-Nr are the **buyer's** keys, HAB/Los/Holzliste the **seller's**, and the Abfuhrfreigabe-Nr links them (TA `Abrechnungs_Nr_Abnehmer`, PTR `Abfuhrfreigabe_Nr`, WDE `Kauf_Nr`). [primary] https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- "Partie-Nr" varies: BaySF Partie = buyer-agreed billing unit; RVWV 8.15 lists "Partienummer" among supplier strings; ELDAT TA calls it "Kundenpartie/Kaufnummer des Käufers" — model as buyer-side free text. "Ladenummer" (plant-assigned load number, as on the pellet sample) could not be sourced; nearest concepts are RVWV's "werkseitig vergebene Zeichenfolge", LSK `Lieferschein_Nr_EMPF` and `Abnehmerreferenz_Nr`. [unverified] https://rvr-deutschland.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/DFWR_DeSH_RVWV_Anlage_8_15_250513.pdf · https://kwf2020.kwf-online.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ELDAT_Satzarten_211.xls
- Vocabulary: ELDAT says Transportauftrag ("Nr des Abfuhrauftrags"); BaySF issues Einzelfrachtaufträge and calls the e-version Transportauftrag; FNR/LWF use Fuhrauftrag for the per-trip order. Provision-notice names: Bereitstellungsanzeige (ForstBW), Bereitstellungsmeldung/Holzabfuhrschein (RLP), Lieferanzeige (classic LA), Holzbereitstellungsanzeige (`hba`). [primary] https://www.fnr.de/fileadmin/projektdatenbank/22022714.pdf · https://www.lwf.bayern.de/mam/cms04/forsttechnik-holz/dateien/glossar_projekt_anforderungen_moderne_it.pdf

### 1.5 Lieferschein minimum content and customer rules
- **RVWV Anlage 8.15** "Informationsgehalt von Frachtpapieren bei Lieferung von Stammholz zur Werksvermessung" (DFWR/DeSH, 13.05.2025, in force 01.10.2025): one Lieferschein per (Teil-)Lieferung, electronic allowed. Minimum 8.15.1: (1) Lieferscheinnummer (2) Verladeort (3) Abnehmer (4) Lieferant (5) Spedition (6) driver name (7) mill-assigned identification key (8) Schätzmenge (9) Holzarten (10) Abladeort (11) Annahmedatum (12) Empfangsbestätigung des Abnehmers. Optional 8.15.2: supplier codes (HAB/Los, Bereitstellungs-, Verkaufslos-, Partie-, Chargen-, Fuhren-Nummer), Kennzeichen tractor/trailer or wagon, Verladedatum/-uhrzeit, several partial deliveries flag, Polternummer/-ID, "Polterabfuhr abgeschlossen und ggf. verbliebene Restmenge", Stückzahl, Sorte, Länge, driver signature. Not checked in certification. [primary] https://rvr-deutschland.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/DFWR_DeSH_RVWV_Anlage_8_15_250513.pdf
- **BaySF Leistungsbeschreibung Rundholztransporte** H-FB-500 (01/2019): orders "per Email oder in Ausnahmefällen per Fax" with Lageplan and Übersichtskarte; "Eine Abfuhr ohne BaySF-Lieferschein ist untersagt", filled in before leaving the loading site; per note: Empfänger, Abfuhrdatum/-uhrzeit, Lieferbetrieb, Revier, Kd-Partie-Nr, Polterauftrags-/Partienummer, HAB, Los; paper notes faxed/e-mailed daily; e-handover by digital signature, external receipt number or receiver's name; freight "grundsätzlich nach Werksmaß", Gutschrift every 14 days; minimum 25 Fm per order, 15 Fm per Polter; invoices without original notes are returned. The Frachtauftrag plus filled Lieferschein "dienen … als Warenbegleitpapiere gemäß § 7 GüKG". [primary] https://www.baysf.de/sites/default/files/doc/leistungsbeschreibung-rundholztransporte.pdf
- BaySF VZB-WV: for Werksmaß sales the Partie is "die zwingend notwendige Zuordnungs-, Abwicklungs- und Abrechnungseinheit"; one Partie per truck/wagon; one Lieferschein per load, mixed loads need one note per Partie. [primary] https://www.baysf.de/sites/default/files/doc/werksvermessungsverkauf.pdf
- **ForstBW AVZ-H 2021**: wood recorded "losweise in Holzlisten"; piles marked with Holzlistennummer + consecutive Polter-Nummer; "Die Holzlisten müssen die GPS-Koordinaten der Polter beinhalten"; haul-off only after Abfuhrfreigabe, which must be carried and shown on demand; Los marking stays until fully hauled; frei Werk: receiver confirms on the carried Lieferschein, risk passes with that confirmation. QA Rundholztransport 02.2025: pile markings must survive partial haul-off. [primary] https://www.forstbw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Bilder_ForstBW/Produkte_und_Angebote/Holz/Verkaufs-_und_Zahlungsbedingungen/ForstBW_AVZ-H_2021.pdf · https://www.forstbw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/QA_Rundholztransport_02.25.pdf
- **Landesforsten RLP HVA V2.2** (2017): haulier notifies the Revierleiter one day before haul-off; "Grundsätzlich ist nur schlagweise abzufahren"; mixed loads need separate marking and weighing. [primary] https://d-nb.info/1227673035/34
- Whether a mill may refuse unloading without a complete note is a matter of its Einkaufsbedingungen; "confirm, then unload/measure" is implied, not mandated. [secondary] https://www.baysf.de/sites/default/files/doc/leistungsbeschreibung-rundholztransporte.pdf · https://rvr-deutschland.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/DFWR_DeSH_RVWV_Anlage_8_15_250513.pdf

### 1.6 Statute: § 7 GüKG, § 408 HGB, 40 t / 44 t, load securing
- **§ 7 GüKG**: commercial haulage must carry the licence, technical proofs and "ein Begleitpapier oder ein sonstiger Nachweis, in dem die beförderten Güter, der Be- und Entladeort und der Auftraggeber angegeben sind"; electronic documents allowed if authenticity, confidentiality and integrity are secured. [primary] https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/g_kg_1998/__7.html
- **§ 408 HGB** Frachtbrief (consignment note, optional): 12 items incl. sender, carrier, receiver, take-over/delivery place and date, goods designation, "Rohgewicht oder die anders angegebene Menge", freight; three signed originals; electronic record equivalent. No statute prescribes a Lieferschein. [primary] https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hgb/__408.html ; [secondary] https://logistik-kompakt.de/lieferschein-pflichtangaben/
- **§ 34 StVZO**: combinations with more than four axles max 40,00 t; 44,00 t only for intermodal container/swap-body transport; driven axle 11,5 t; +1 t alternative drive, +2 t zero-emission. **§ 70 StVZO**: higher administrative authorities may grant exemptions, area-limited, proof carried on board. [primary] https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__34.html · https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__70.html
- **Baden-Württemberg Schadholzerlass** (23.01.2024): hauliers may apply for "bis zu 44 Tonnen" for damaged timber, valid "bis zum 31. Dezember 2027", bridges excluded, "keine dauerhafte Lösung". AGR overview (Jan 2025) gives BW to 31.03.2027 with bridge negative list, Hessen/NRW 44 t to 31.12.2025, Thüringen 41,8 t, others expired — dates after 31.12.2025 not re-verified. [primary]/[secondary] https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/service/presse/pressemitteilung/pid/ausnahmeregelung-fuer-schwerere-schadholztransporte · https://www.digitalmagazin.de/heftbilder/forsttechnik/forst-technik-2025-03/agr-ausnahmegenehmigungen-kalamitaetsholztransport-48355277.pdf
- Consequence: a 44 t permit holder weighed above 44 t falls back to the 40 t rule (police case 50 300 kg = +25,75 %); max height 4 m; overloads > 25 % are fined for driver and company. [secondary] https://www.lasiportal.de/anderes/rohholztransporte-haben-eigene-regeln/
- **Load securing**: joint "Verladeempfehlung für Rohholz" quer/längs (09.10.2006, NRW ministries, police, BAG, GDV, AGR, BdHG, BGL, DFWR … based on VDI 2700): stanchions ≥ ca. 20 cm above the outer logs, stacks ≤ 7 m, ≥ 2 lashings cross-loaded, each length-loaded stack on ≥ 2 Rungenpaare, "Ein auf die Ladung gelegter Ladekran darf nicht mit eingespannt werden"; in force since 15.10.2006; VDI 2700 is an "anerkannte Regel der Technik", not law. ### 1.7 Wiegeschein and Messprotokoll content
- **Wiegeschein** (weighing slip) per ForstBW ZVZ-IG § 7: running number; Forstbezirk; Revier; Abfuhrfreigabe-Nr; Waldort; Holzlistennummer; Polternummer(n); "teilw." flag for partial haul-off; Kennzeichen (Motorwagen and trailer); Fuhrunternehmer/Fahrer; date/time of mill entry; **Brutto, Tara, Netto, Trockengehalt, Trockengewicht (Atro)**; last slip of a billing unit marked "Rest"; "jeder Wiegeschein muss zweifelsfrei einer bestimmten Abfuhrfreigabe zugeordnet werden können"; bridge scale 50 t / 20 kg. - Public-forest sales terms: driver receives the signed original Wiegeschein at the scale; data to be given at the scale at once. [secondary] https://www.landesforsten.de/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/hvz_01.01.2015aktuelle-adresse.pdf
- **Messprotokoll** (mill measurement record): RVWV 2026 (signed 15.05.2026, recommended from 01.07.2026) moves the billing diameter to the physical middle (PMD, "fester Winkel"), keeps Sortenlänge and SMD for grading, adds Anlage 8.8 (protocol design) and 8.17 (Teilstammerfassung); per (Teil-)Lieferung an Einzelstammprotokoll plus Summenprotokoll whose header carries "Lieferscheinnummer" and "Stückzahl: entsprechend Angabe LKW-Fahrer". ### 1.8 Units and RVR conversion factors
- RVR 2020 Table 4: Fm o.R. (Festmeter ohne Rinde, solid m³ under bark) for all assortments; Rm m.R. (Raummeter mit Rinde, stacked m³) and t atro m.R. for industrial/energy wood; SRm (Schüttraummeter) for chips; "1 Fm o.R. [wird] dem Festgehaltsvolumen von 1 m³ gleichgesetzt". [primary] https://rvr-deutschland.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Rahmenvereinbarung_fuer_den_Rohholzhandel_in_Deutschland.pdf
- Table 5 (1/2/3 m pieces): 1 Rm m.R. = 0,70 / 0,65 / 0,60 Fm o.R.; 1 Rm o.R. = 0,80 / 0,75 / 0,70; 1 SRm ≙ 0,4 Fm o.R. Table 6 (1 t atro → Fm o.R.): Eiche 1,4 · Buche 1,5 (to be revised) · Pappel 2,2 · Hartlaub 1,6 · Weichlaub 1,9 · Fichte/Tanne 2,2 · Kiefer 2,1 · Douglasie 2,1 · Lärche 1,8; HVA RLP Tab. 26 matches ("gültig ab Sommer 2016"). [primary] same PDF; https://d-nb.info/1227673035/34
- Caveat verbatim: "Umrechnungsfaktoren … dienen vorrangig statistischen Zwecken … Über Umrechnungsfaktoren hergeleitete Werte sind als Verkaufsmaß nicht anzuwenden." Measure hierarchy (HVA): Verkaufsmaß / Kontrollmaß / Dispositionsmaß (e.g. Harvestermaß — fine for transport orders, "nicht als Verkaufsmaß"). [primary] https://rvr-deutschland.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Rahmenvereinbarung_fuer_den_Rohholzhandel_in_Deutschland.pdf (§ 4.2) · https://d-nb.info/1227673035/34 (p.5)
- Why factors must be versioned: RVR plans to change the Buche factor; HVA dates its table; RVWV 2026 changed the billing-measure definition itself; ELDAT attaches `conversion_factor_qm` per amount and a per-Hieb `conversion_factor_qm_t`. [primary] https://rvr-deutschland.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Rahmenvereinbarung_fuer_den_Rohholzhandel_in_Deutschland.pdf · https://d-nb.info/1227673035/34 · https://rvr-deutschland.de/rvwv/ · https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kwf-ev/eldat-schema/1.0.4/schema_de.json

### 1.9 Hieb / Los / Polter conventions per state
- **Baden-Württemberg (ForstBW, FBG glossary)**: Holzliste per Los is "eine Urkunde"; Holzlistennummer = Forstwirtschaftsjahr + Reviernummer + Aufnahmenummer + Losnummer; Aufnahme = Hieb with recording, numbered per Revier; Los = sales unit, "immer sortenrein", one owner, several Polter; Polternummer consecutive within the Aufnahme; Abfuhrfreigabe is a separate numbered document (classic LA `Vertrag_Nr` "In Baden-Württemberg eine vom System vergebene Nummer"); industrial wood: one Abfuhrfreigabe per Abrechnungseinheit "(i.d.R. für jeden Hieb)". [primary]/[secondary] https://fbg-landkreis-tuttlingen.de/holzverkauf-fachbegriffe/ · ForstBW AVZ-H and ZVZ-IG above
- **Rheinland-Pfalz (HVA)**: recording unit is the HAB-Los (Holzaufnahmebuch-Los), any number of Polter per HAB-Los; buyer documents reference Kaufvertragsnummer + laufende Vorzeigungsnummer; piles carry Polterplättchen with Polternummer and Stückzahl; mill piece counts reconciled to HAB-Los counts. [primary] https://d-nb.info/1227673035/34
- **Bayern (BaySF)**: Partie is the buyer-agreed billing unit, Partienummern assigned jointly; Waldkontrollmaß per Partie for the 80 % advance invoice; notes carry Kd-Partie-Nr, Polterauftrags-/Partienummer, HAB, Los. [primary] https://www.baysf.de/sites/default/files/doc/werksvermessungsverkauf.pdf · https://www.baysf.de/sites/default/files/doc/leistungsbeschreibung-rundholztransporte.pdf
- LWF Polterdatenbank record: Polter-Nr (unique key) | Geokoordinaten | Baumart | Sorte | Länge | Menge | Waldbesitzer | Kunde | Status, with Restmenge when a pile is not finished. [primary] https://www.lwf.bayern.de/mam/cms04/forsttechnik-holz/dateien/glossar_projekt_anforderungen_moderne_it.pdf

### 1.11 NavLog licensing
- GeoDat is the forest-road data standard (DFWR/DHWR, coordinated by KWF, since 2004; spec "Pragmatisches ShapeForst 3.3"). NavLog GmbH (Groß-Umstadt; shareholders AGDW, DStGB, KWF, AGR) builds the routable nationwide forest-road dataset: > 560 000 km, ~2,8 M segments, ~560 k point objects (status 06/2025). [primary] https://digitalisierung.fnr.de/datenstandards/geodat · https://navlog.de/informationen/ · https://bks-portal.rlp.de/system/files/gruppen/1411/dateien/navlog_praesentation_bos.pdf

NRW free for private/municipal owners and SMEs of the timber industry (2012); Hessen (2023) covers forest & timber firms < 100 000 FM/yr; RLP and BW joined BY and NRW in 2021 with the Plus-Option for "Speditionen, Forstliche Lohnunternehmen, Holzhandel". Several navlog.de licence pages were 404 on 16.10.2026. [primary]/[secondary] https://navlog.de/landeslizenz-hessen/ · https://fuv-rlp.de/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/DL-intern_2021-01_FUV-RLP_NavLog.pdf · https://www.waldbauernverband.de/2016/navlog.php
- Open substitute: none classifies German forest roads for timber trucks; OSM `highway=track` + `tracktype` + `access=forestry` has good track coverage but inconsistent truck attributes. [secondary] https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/DE:Tag:highway=track · https://media.ccc.de/v/fossgis2026-84163-wie-zuverlassig-sind-openstreetmap-daten-fur-lkw-routing

### 2.5 Pain points
- **Waiting at the mill**: queues at opening and 6–9 h "bis hin zu einer Stunde", peaks 11 h and 14 h; rail unloading stops truck intake; "Von den befragten Werken nutzt keines aktuell Zeitfenster"; hauliers estimate **+11 % annual volume** without waiting; "Wartezeiten beim Abnehmer werden nicht vergütet" (BdHG 2017); Mercer Holz books slots via app for ~500 trucks/day (2024). [primary] https://www.lfl.bayern.de/mam/cms04/service/dateien/marktstudie_rundholzlogistik_bf.pdf (pp. 17, 46–47) · https://de.mercerint.com/neues/2024/digitalisierung-holzlogistik/ ; [secondary] https://www.holzlogistik.iff.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/iff/holzlogistik/de/dokumente/veranstaltungen/ressource-holz-2017-burkhardt.pdf
- **Finding the Polter**: hand-typed "Zahlendreher" → "Suchfahrten"; "Poltersuche sei immer wieder ein Problem … wenn die Lagekoordinaten falsch sind oder das Polter auf der Karte falsch eingezeichnet"; search trips are not paid. [primary] https://www.lfl.bayern.de/mam/cms04/service/dateien/marktstudie_rundholzlogistik_bf.pdf (pp. 27, 32, 70)
- **Paperwork**: a Lieferschein per load incl. piece count; BaySF paper slips faxed daily, settlement by post; Ladenburger intake took ~15 min of paper handover before terminals. [primary] https://www.baysf.de/sites/default/files/doc/leistungsbeschreibung-rundholztransporte.pdf · https://www.forstzeitung.at/markt/2020/09/effiziente_holzannahme.html
- **44 t**: "Die 44 Tonnen sind ein Witz" (empty truck ~22 t + ~2 t crane; AT 46/50 t, FR 57 t, SE 90 t cited by AGR); 515 bridges barred in BW; exemptions differ per state. [secondary] https://www.eurotransport.de/logistik/verkehrspolitik/holztransport-44-tonnen-sind-ein-witz/ · https://ag-rohholz.de/holztransport-logistik/
- Rates "in den vergangenen 25 Jahren nahezu nicht verändert", 53 h weeks, driver shortage, succession. [primary] https://www.lwf.bayern.de/forsttechnik-holz/logistik/247838/index.php

### 2.6 Mill intake practice
- Standard flow: Holzbereitstellung → Transportauftrag (mill → carrier, accept/reject) → Lieferschein (driver creates, sends at delivery; mill returns "Angenommen") → Messprotokoll (Werksmaß, usually the billing basis) → Abrechnung. [primary] https://digitalisierung.fnr.de/datenstandards/eldatsmart
- Gate systems: Ladenburger touchscreen terminals (ID, name, plate, quantity, pieces, last load; barcode/QR possible; intake from ~15 min to minutes); Holz Schiller (Holtec/Jörg 2025): self-registration terminal, Soll/Ist by QR, LED guidance; Egger Wismar (OAS logis): licence-plate recognition, barcode terminal, two scales, > 500 movements/day; Stora Enso DigiLog: e-Lieferschein from the forest, RFID+QR per partial load, ANPR barrier; Papierholz Austria Amstetten: ANPR + e-Lieferschein, two weighings, ~1 min handover, 30–40 trucks/h. [primary] https://www.holzkurier.com/rundholz/2025/03/von_anlieferung_bisverrechnung.html · https://www.oas.de/success-stories/optimierte-werkszufahrt-mit-holzannahme-am-haffeld-nord · https://www.holzkurier.com/rundholz/2023/12/digitale_rundholzlogistik.html · https://www.holzkurier.com/rundholz/2020/12/automatisierung_undrationalisierung.html
- The driver still typically leaves with a paper/PDF Lieferschein copy or Wiegeschein original, and the Werksmaß arrives ~14 days later via ELDAT or post — the gap the demo's ticket matching and Mengenbuch address. [primary] https://www.lfl.bayern.de/mam/cms04/service/dateien/marktstudie_rundholzlogistik_bf.pdf · https://www.baysf.de/sites/default/files/doc/leistungsbeschreibung-rundholztransporte.pdf · https://www.forstbw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/ForstBW_ZVZ-IG.pdf

### 3.5 Decision table for printed coordinates (normalise first: strip `m`, thousands separators, decimal comma → dot, upper-case)
| Pattern | Interpret as | Check (reject or ask if it fails) |
|---|---|---|
| two decimals, first 47,2–55,1, second 5,8–15,1 | WGS84/ETRS89 lat, lon | inside Germany box; ≥ 4 decimals expected for a Polter |
| two decimals, first 5,8–15,1, second 47,2–55,1 | swapped lon, lat | swap, warn "Reihenfolge Länge/Breite vertauscht" |
| `48°07'24" N 9°07'24" E/O`, `48 07.400 N` | DMS/DMM → decimal | hemisphere letters; minutes and seconds < 60 |
| `32U 512345 5412345`, `32 N …` | UTM 32N (25832) | easting 100 k–900 k (DE ≈ 262–957 k), northing 5,23–6,15 M, band U (T far south) |
| `33U …` | UTM 33N (25833) | easting 100 k–503 k; state east of ~12 °E |
| `32UMV 61344 81745` | UTMREF/MGRS | decode 100 km square letters |
| 8-digit easting starting 32/33 + 7-digit northing | zE-N (4647/5650): strip zone digits | remainder passes UTM checks |
| 7-digit easting starting 2–5 + 7-digit northing | Gauß-Krüger, zone = first digit (3146x) | own strip ± 6 km; zone consistent with state; convert with EPSG:1777, label "≈ 3 m" |
| 6-digit + 7-digit, no zone text | UTM 32N by default for BW/BY/NRW | check easting against the state's range or ask for the zone |
| 7-digit + 6-digit, or northing printed first | swapped axes (Hochwert/Rechtswert order) | swap, re-check, warn |
| passes format but > ~50 km from the Forstrevier | wrong zone or typo | show as unverified, ask to confirm |
Rules of thumb: ≥ 5 200 000 is a northing; a 7-digit value < 5 000 000 is a GK Rechtswert; 6 digits is a UTM easting; 8 digits starting 32/33 is zone-prefixed; anything with a decimal point < 100 is degrees. [secondary: derived from the sources in 3.4]

## §5 Open questions for pilots
- Which ELDATsmart messages, if any, do pilot customers actually send (Transportauftrag JSON vs PDF)? The 2022 survey says almost none for TA/Lieferschein; the mill portals use their own formats. [primary §1.13]
- EUDR on the note: should the Lieferschein carry both `eudr_dds_reference_number` and `eudr_dds_verification_number` per Polter from 30.12.2026, and who supplies them to a carrier that never sees the owner's DDS? [primary §1.12]
- Conversion factors: replace the demo placeholders (rm→fm 0,7; t→fm 1,05) with RVR Table 5/6 values or the customer's per-Hieb `conversion_factor_qm_t`, dated and versioned. [primary §1.8]
- Wiegeschein capture: should the receipt model add Trockengehalt/Atro-Gewicht and the "teilw."/"Rest" flags of ForstBW ZVZ-IG and HVA RLP? [primary §1.7]
- Which note-number regime applies per customer — carrier's own range, `delivery_no_range[]` from the Transportauftrag (LSN), or the mill's Annahmenummer? [primary §1.2–1.3]
- Partie vs HAB/Los vs Holzliste keys per customer; the meaning of "Ladenummer" at pellet/industrial plants. [unverified §1.4]
- Coordinates: confirm with each customer whether lists print UTM 32 without zone, zone-prefixed eastings, GK or DMS, and set the "far from region" radius (demo 50 km). [secondary §3.4–3.5]
- Weights: whether to carry a 44 t § 70 StVZO exemption document per truck and state with its expiry (BW 31.12.2027 vs 31.03.2027 per AGR). [primary/secondary §1.6]
- Map data: NavLog Plus-Option availability and cost for a 1–3-truck haulier in BW/BY/NRW; whether the pin popup should link to Rettungspunkte; OSM tracks are not truck-classified. [primary/secondary §1.11]
- Basemap: switch from OSM to basemap.de (CC BY 4.0) with the required credit if demo traffic grows; the WMS orthophotos of BW/BY/NRW are available key-free. [primary §3.2–3.3]
- Driver device: real offline storage (IndexedDB + `navigator.storage.persist()`), queue drain without Background Sync on iOS, and the local-data expiry setting. [primary §3.7]
- Quantity-difference tolerance (demo 0,5 %), waiting-time logging as billing evidence (+11 % volume claim), and whether a mill may refuse unloading without a complete note. [primary/secondary §2.5, §1.5]
